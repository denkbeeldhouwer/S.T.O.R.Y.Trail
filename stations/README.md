# Stations

One directory per station, numbered by its place in the walk. Each holds the station's design, its siting — why here, and what the place already carries — and the reflection question a walker meets there.

Twelve stations, and the route itself is the thirteenth. Four are small reflection moments, five are mid-size interactive works, three are icons. The circle at Presikhaaf is the elixir circle.

A station's directory is added once its design is settled enough to build from.

S.T.O.R.Y.-Trail

A 9.5 km walking trail of living willow, from Arnhem Centraal to Park Presikhaaf — between two campuses.

The trail is designed and drawn. Nothing is in the ground yet.

Twelve installations stand along the route, each a step in the journey of the hero, and a thirteenth is the walk itself. The willow grows, changes with the seasons, and is pruned each winter by the students who learn nearby and the people who live there.

The walk

The unit of change is not distance but angle. Twelve stations, three hundred and sixty degrees, thirty degrees apart — TwistStep. You end where you began and see something else.

We build backwards. The elixir circle at Presikhaaf, the end of the journey, is built first, next to the campuses. From there the trail is laid back toward the station. Understand backwards, live forwards.

Living willow

Willow is not a material that is finished. It runs out every spring and is cut back every winter. Maintenance is not a burden added to the work; it is the work, continuing. Each installation has someone who tends it.

The first icons are built by a master together with students and residents, who then build and maintain the rest themselves. A guild is healthy when its members grow less dependent on the master, not more.

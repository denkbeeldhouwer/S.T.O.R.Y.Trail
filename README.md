# S.T.O.R.Y.-Trail

A walking trail of living willow, 9.5 km from station to station. From Arnhem Centraal to Presikhaaf, between two campuses. Twelve installations stand along it, each a step in the journey of the hero, and a thirteenth is the walk itself. The willow grows, changes with the seasons, and is pruned each winter by the students who learn nearby and the people who live there.

The trail is designed and drawn. Nothing is in the ground yet.

## The walk

The unit of change is not distance but angle. Twelve stations, three hundred and sixty degrees, thirty degrees apart — *TwistStep*. You end where you began and see something else.

Four stations are small reflection moments, five are mid-size interactive works, three are icons: the threshold, the ordeal, and the elixir circle. The rhythm is deliberate, and the crescendo is the point.

We build backwards. The elixir circle at Presikhaaf, the end of the journey, is built first, next to the campuses. From there the trail is laid back toward the station. Søren Kierkegaard: 'Life Can Only Be Understood Backwards, But It Must Be Lived Forwards'

## Living willow

Willow is not a material that is finished. It runs out every spring and is cut back every winter. Maintenance is not a burden added to the work; it is the work, continuing. Each installation has someone who tends it.

The first icons are built by a master together with students and residents, who then build and maintain the rest themselves. A guild is healthy when its members grow less dependent on the master, not more.

## Building one elsewhere

This repository holds what a stranger would need to build a version of this in another city: the concept, the twelve stations and their spatial design, the route, and the knowledge of how living willow is grown, pruned, and kept safe. It does not hold the names of the people who made it happen here — those live elsewhere, and appear only once they have agreed to it.

What went wrong is documented as carefully as what worked. A trail that is never finished has no finished documentation either.
